###  Featured Data Projects

#### 1️⃣ [EV Battery Safety & Failure Prediction (20K Records)](https://github.com/dorsafmwissaoui/EV_Battery_Safety_Analysis)
* **Objective:** Analyzed 70 engineering and operational telemetry features of electric vehicle batteries to uncover root causes of degradation and thermal runaway risks.
* **Key Impact:** Trained a **Balanced Random Forest Classifier** achieving **94.38% accuracy** in predicting battery failures, and identified maximum cell temperature (**52.51°C** vs. **39.47°C** in healthy units) as a primary failure driver.
* **Tools:** `Python`, `Pandas`, `Scikit-Learn`, `Tableau Public`.

#### 2️⃣ [Electric Vehicle (EV) Market Analysis & ML Forecasting (135K+ Records)](https://github.com/dorsafmwissaoui/EV_Market_Data_Analysis)
* **Objective:** Evaluated EV adoption growth, manufacturer market shares, and battery range tiers across Washington State, USA.
* **Key Impact:** Uncovered **Tesla's 45.85% market dominance**, built regression and classification models (**R² = 99.59%** for battery range estimation), and forecasted market adoption through **2028**.
* **Tools:** `Python`, `Pandas`, `Scikit-Learn`, `Tableau Public`, `Google Sheets`.

#### 3️⃣ [Tunisian Agricultural Cooperatives (SMSA) Data Analysis](https://github.com/dorsafmwissaoui/Tunisia-Agriculture-SMSA-Pipeline)
* **Objective:** Cleaned, standardized, and analyzed government registry data for agricultural cooperatives in Tunisia to evaluate capital distribution and farmer engagement.
* **Key Impact:** Standardized inconsistent regional naming and currency scales, handled missing capital values via regional median imputation, and proved via **Pearson correlation** that cooperative membership is driven by proximity rather than capital size.
* **Tools:** `Python`, `Pandas`, `Statistical Analysis (IQR & Pearson)`, `Google Sheets`.
